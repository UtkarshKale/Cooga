# Research & Reference Links

Compact index of the research sources discussed for the two posters. Links are clean publisher/arXiv URLs. These support a literature-based synthesis; they do **not** mean the full text of every paper was read in this chat.

## A. Agentic AI — core sources

1. **“A Survey on LLM Agents: Architecture, Applications, and Challenges.”** IEEE ICOIN, 2026. [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11480623/) — Survey of agent architecture, including memory, planning, tool use, control flows, evaluation, reliability and scalability.
2. **Arunkumar V., Gangadharan G. R., Buyya R. — “Agentic Artificial Intelligence (AI): Architectures, Taxonomies, and Evaluation of Large Language Model Agents.”** arXiv, 2026. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12560) — Taxonomy covering perception, planning, action, tools, collaboration, evaluation and failure modes.
3. **Mamdouh Alenezi — “From Prompt-Response to Goal-Directed Systems: The Evolution of Agentic AI Software Architecture.”** arXiv, 2026. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10479) — Production architecture, typed tool interfaces, multi-agent topologies, governance and observability.
4. **Sheng Cao et al. — “The Auton Agentic AI Framework.”** arXiv, 2026. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23720) — Separates an agent's cognitive blueprint from runtime execution; covers memory, MCP, safety and runtime optimization.
5. **Yi Yu et al. — “Agentic Memory: Learning Unified Long-Term and Short-Term Memory Management for Large Language Model Agents.”** ACL, 2026. [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.981/) · [PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.981.pdf) — AgeMem lets agents choose when to store, retrieve, update, summarize and discard memory.
6. **“Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Agent-to-Agent (A2A) Protocol for Scalable Agentic AI Systems.”** IEEE ICICDS, 2026. [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11604962/) — MCP for standardized tool/data access; A2A for agent communication and coordination.
7. **“LLM-Based Intelligent Agents for Cybersecurity: A Tutorial and Survey of Automated Vulnerability Discovery.”** IEEE Access, 2026. [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11580353/) — Tool-using agents for vulnerability discovery and testing; discusses reliability, cost and ethical issues.
8. **“A Survey of Fuzzing Techniques for Large Language Model Agents: Challenges, Methods, and Future Directions.”** IEEE BDAI, 2026. [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11655176/) — Agent security/testing risks including indirect prompt injection, planning hallucination and tool abuse.
9. **“GLaMP: A Grounded Language Model-Based Multiagent System for Long-Horizon Robotic Task Planning in Industrial Settings.”** IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics, 2026. [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11641677/) — VLM + LLM planning, symbolic grounding, feedback and failure-aware replanning.
10. **“Beyond Single-Framework Architectures: A Systematic Evaluation and Hybrid Design for Scalable Multi-Agent Coordination.”** IEEE Access, 2026. [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11481053/) — Evaluates CrewAI, AutoGen, LangChain and a hybrid design. Reported performance numbers apply to the paper's own benchmark, not universally.

### Industry / product references (not peer-reviewed papers)
- **Anthropic, “How we built our multi-agent research system.”** June 2025. [Engineering blog](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system) — Orchestrator/worker architecture, parallel research agents, evaluation, context management and token-cost trade-offs.
- **OpenAI, “GPT-6 Astra: A new generation of intelligence.”** [Official announcement](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/) · [API model documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-astra) — Product/technical documentation; do not present as a peer-reviewed paper.

## B. AI in Disaster Management — verified sources

1. **S. Muthukumar et al. — “A systematic review of artificial intelligence frameworks for holistic disaster management.”** *Discover Artificial Intelligence*, 6, article 308, 2026. [Publisher page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44163-026-01020-w) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44163-026-01020-w) — Synthesizes 96 peer-reviewed studies across disaster-management phases and AI approaches.
2. **“A systematic review of trustworthy artificial intelligence applications in natural disasters.”** *Computers & Electrical Engineering*, 118B, 109409, 2024. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compeleceng.2024.109409) — AI for prediction, risk assessment, early warning and evacuation, with emphasis on trustworthy deployment.
3. **Akhyar et al. — “Deep artificial intelligence applications for natural disaster management systems: A methodological review.”** *Ecological Indicators*, 163, 112067, 2024. [Publisher page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1470160X24005247) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolind.2024.112067) — Reviews AI applications for forest fires, earthquakes and floods.
4. **“Artificial intelligence for flood risk management: A comprehensive state-of-the-art review and future directions.”** *International Journal of Disaster Risk Reduction*, 117, 105110, 2025. [Publisher page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2212420924008720) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijdrr.2024.105110) — Reviews ML/DL models, input data and applications in flood-risk management.
5. **“Survey of AI-Enabled IoT for Wildfire Prediction, Detection, and Post-Operations.”** *IEEE Access*, 14, 9819–9837, 2026. [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11355433/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3654863) — AIoT for wildfire prediction, real-time detection, damage assessment and recovery.
6. **Zhenyu Lei et al. — “Harnessing Large Language Models for Disaster Management: A Survey.”** Findings of ACL, 2025, pp. 14528–14551. [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.750/) · [PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.750.pdf) — Taxonomy of LLM uses across disaster phases; discusses datasets, use cases and challenges.
7. **Zhaohui Chen et al. — “Integration of large vision language models for efficient post-disaster damage assessment and reporting.”** *Nature Communications*, 2026. [Nature Communications](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-68216-z) — Introduces **DisasTeller**, coordinating four specialized vision-language agents for assessment, alerts, emergency reports, resource allocation and recovery planning.
